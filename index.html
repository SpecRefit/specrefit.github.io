FROM SPECIFICATION TO PROJECT

Keep the contract.
Make the choices explicit.

Contract changes should be understandable, reviewable and reproducible.

01 / INSPECT

See how it fits together.

Explore operations, schemas, references and security requirements. Understand what goes in, what comes out and where the contract needs attention.

02 / REFIT

Choose what you need.

Start with JSON preferences and explicit exceptions. Review the effect of each rule before exporting an adapted specification.

03 / REPEAT

Carry your decisions forward.

Keep the rules in your project. Reapply them to incoming contract versions, with clear feedback when something no longer matches.

DESIGNED FOR YOUR TOOLCHAIN

Visual choices.
Repeatable builds.

The editor will save a specrefit.yaml configuration. The planned Maven plugin will apply those rules during your build and hand the resulting OpenAPI contract to Fabrikt or another generator.

The output remains an OpenAPI specification.

YOUR CONTRACTS, YOUR ENVIRONMENT

Local comes first.

We are designing SpecRefit for standalone use on Windows, macOS and Linux, without telemetry or a required account. The optional web editor is planned to process contracts in your browser.

Explicitly configured references may use your network. Internal hosting and local execution remain part of the design.
